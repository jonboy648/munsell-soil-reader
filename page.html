PROTOTYPE: Photo to Munsell soil color

The question: can a phone photo give a trustworthy Munsell soil reading, and how much does a color card in the shot matter? Every reading below is computed three ways from the same click: straight from the photo, with white balance from the card's gray patches, and with a full correction fitted to all 24 card patches.

Throwaway prototype, not the app. The test photos are made by a simulated camera, which is simpler than a real one, so real photos will do somewhat worse than the test photos. Card: X-Rite ColorChecker Classic (24 patches). Soil chips: an approximate soil-book grid (7 hues from 10R to 5Y, values 2.5 to 8, chromas 1 to 8), not the official chip list.
